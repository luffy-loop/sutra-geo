PROTOTYPE MODERATION

Review queue

A production version would restrict this queue to authorized moderators.

Accuracy firstClaims about a place, date or tradition should be checkable against a source or a named local voice, not presented as certain when it isn't.
Respectful of communitiesStories about living communities, festivals or rituals should not mock, stereotype or misrepresent the people they describe.
No unverifiable sensitive claimsSubmissions making medical, legal or safety claims are rejected and redirected to an official source.
Consent for peoplePhotos or stories naming identifiable individuals should have their awareness or consent noted in the submission.
